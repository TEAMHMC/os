The problem

The services exist. Reaching them is the barrier.

A person who needs housing, food or mental health support in Los Angeles is not short of programs. They are short of a way in.

What it takes today

Knowing which agency covers your need, which one covers your area, whether they take your insurance, and whether they are still operating. Then calling during business hours and waiting.

Who it filters out

Anyone without a data plan, a stable address, spare daytime hours, or English. Which is a large share of the people the system is meant to serve.

Most digital health tools assume a browser, a login and patience. We started from the opposite assumption.

How it works

Grounded, not remembered.

1

A person texts or calls the Twilio number in their own words.

2

Twilio Messaging or Voice delivers it to our service as a signed webhook.

3

We search our directory of vetted community organizations for the actual need.

4

Gemini on Vertex AI writes the answer from what was retrieved, in the language they used.

5

SendGrid and Programmable Messaging carry the follow up.

Why grounding is the whole point. A language model asked about housing in South Los Angeles will produce a confident, plausible answer. Some of it will be wrong, and a wrong phone number given to someone in crisis is worse than no answer. Every response names an organization we vetted, or says plainly that nothing matched.

5

Safety

Crisis detection runs before the model, not inside it.

Every inbound message is checked on our own servers against a crisis pattern list before any AI is called.

If it matches

The person receives the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline immediately, from our own code. It is logged and alerted. No model decides whether somebody is in danger.

If it does not

The message proceeds to retrieval and the navigator answers normally.

We designed the failure path before the feature path. On a public health line that ordering is the product decision that matters most.

Roadmap

Our directory is the first source, not the only one.

We vet the organizations we list, which is why the navigator can be trusted when it names one. But a need we do not cover should still return a real place to call.

findhelp.org

Local clinics, prescription help and wellness programs by area

HRSA Find a Health Center

Federally qualified health centers that see people regardless of insurance status

MedlinePlus directories

Specialist care, including cancer centers, dialysis and mental health services

The navigator will search these alongside our own directory, filtered to trusted sources and always citing where an answer came from, then narrow by the need and the postcode the person gives.

9

Differentiation

Why this is hard to copy.

We run the directory

We are not an interface over somebody else data. We vet the organizations, so when the navigator names one it is a place we would send a person ourselves.

Phone first, genuinely

SMS and voice are not fallbacks added late. They are the primary channel, in two languages, with no account required.

We are the operator

We run street medicine and events ourselves, so the platform is shaped by the work rather than guessed at from outside it.

Safety is architectural

Crisis routing sits above the model in the call stack, where it cannot be talked out of firing.

Compliance

Built to the standard the work requires.

Data handling

Clinical screening data is stripped before any external model call. Audit logging on member records, with retention set to seven years.

Infrastructure

Runs in our own Google Cloud project under our existing agreement. Secrets in Secret Manager, signed webhooks, rate limiting on public endpoints.

We are building to HIPAA and SOC 2 standards. Certification is outstanding and we describe it that way.

Guidance on handling protected health information across Twilio channels is one of the things we would most value from this programme.
