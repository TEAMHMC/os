The loop

Follow one person all the way through.

Nine tools only matter if they hand off to each other. Here is the handoff, in the order it happens.

Step one

Someone shows up.

A screening at a park, an event found by zip code, or a private check-in at eleven at night. All three start the same record.

EventFinder, CheckYourself
Step two

A trained volunteer takes the visit.

Their orientation, their credential, their shift and their hours are already on file, so who ran the visit is never a question afterwards.

HMC VMS, HMC Academy
Step three

A referral goes out.

Routed to a partner by the services that partner declared. Housing to housing. Behavioral health to behavioral health. Not a flyer and a hope.

Resource Directory, Partner Portal
Step four

The partner closes it.

Accept or decline, serve the person, then record the outcome and how many people it covered. Nothing sits open with nobody watching.

Partner Portal
Step five

The result comes back.

Outcomes, hours and completions land in one place, so a report is pulled from the work rather than rebuilt from memory a quarter later.

The work under the software.

Every tool on this page was built for a program that was already running. Here is what those programs are answerable to.

County funding

Named in the CalMHSA Intent to Award for LACDMH Community Grants, through a competitive RFP.

Continuing education

Unstoppable™ approved by LACDMH on February 27 2026 for 1.0 CE and CEU across BBS, BRN, CCAPP and psychology.

Field research

A February 2025 Skid Row study of 75 respondents, certified exempt by the UCLA Institutional Review Board.

Community design

Mobile Health Outreach was built from a community needs assessment of 169 respondents, who named cost, time and transportation as what stops them.

Standing

California 501(c)(3), EIN 85-3784250, founded 2020, certified as an LA County Social Enterprise.

Federal registration

Active in SAM.gov, Grants.gov and eRA Commons, so a federal award has nothing to wait on.

How the data is held

Access is controlled and logged, and data is encrypted in transit and at rest. HMC OS™ is built to HIPAA and SOC 2 standards.

What happens next

Three ways in.

The first is open to anyone this minute. The other two start with a conversation and end with you on the system rather than next to it.

For anyone who needs support

Everything on this page that is open to the public sits behind one door. No application, no cost, and nothing to prove before you can use it.

  1. Enter an email address at the Member Hub.
  2. Take a private check-in, or find an event near you.
  3. Ask for help with food, housing, care or transport.
Enter the Member Hub

In a crisis, call or text 988, the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, any time of day.

For partner organizations

Reach the whole county with your events, receive referrals matched to what you actually do, and close each one on the record.

  1. Tell us the services your organization provides.
  2. Get an account on the Partner Portal.
  3. Start taking referrals and posting your events.
Partner with us

Or write to partner@healthmatters.clinic.

For funders

Fund a system that already runs. Events, referrals, volunteer hours and training completions are captured where they happen, so reporting comes out of the work instead of being rebuilt after it.

  1. Walk the tools with us, live and with real data.
  2. Ask for the numbers behind any program on this page.
  3. Fund a named piece of the system and watch it report.
